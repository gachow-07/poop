# Fridge to Table

A single-page meal planner. List what's in your fridge, mark anything that needs using first, choose days, meals, servings, diet and cook time, and get a meal plan with steps and a shopping list.

- **Inside Claude** (the published artifact), Claude writes the plan around your exact ingredients and can read a photo of your fridge.
- **Opened anywhere else** (just open `index.html` in a browser), it falls back to a built-in recipe matcher that works offline.

Your fridge list is saved in your browser's local storage.
